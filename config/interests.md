I care about the semiconductor industry with a strong emphasis on analog, RF, and
mixed-signal circuit design: new data converter and PLL architectures, RF front ends,
power management ICs, and the business of analog-heavy companies like TI, ADI, and
Skyworks. I follow AI hardware closely: GPU and accelerator announcements, datacenter
silicon from NVIDIA, AMD, Google, and startups, HBM and advanced packaging, and the
economics of AI compute. I am especially interested in AI applied to chip design:
machine learning in EDA tools, automated analog layout, placement and routing with
reinforcement learning, and what Synopsys, Cadence, and academic groups are doing there.
Foundry news (TSMC, Samsung, Intel Foundry), process node progress, and chip startup
funding rounds matter to me. I care less about consumer gadget reviews, gaming benchmarks,
crypto mining, and general business news that only mentions chips in passing.
