My three focus areas, in priority order:

1. AI APPLIED TO ANALOG AND RF CHIP DESIGN (highest priority)
Machine learning for automated analog layout, circuit sizing and optimization with
reinforcement learning, generative models for schematic synthesis, ML-driven
parasitic-aware design, Bayesian optimization for analog tuning, neural network-based
modeling of transistors and passives. What Synopsys, Cadence, academic research groups,
and startups are doing to bring AI/ML into the analog and RF design flow. Papers and
news in this area should always be included.

2. COMPUTE FOR RUNNING AI IN DESIGN WORKFLOWS
I am an analog designer who wants to run AI/ML models to help me design circuits. I
care about the GPUs, workstations, cloud compute, and infrastructure that let me train
and run these models: new NVIDIA and AMD GPU launches, GPU pricing and availability,
cloud GPU instances, on-prem AI workstation options, and ML framework support (PyTorch,
JAX). I care about this as a user of AI compute, not as a chip designer building AI
hardware. Stories about AI chip packaging, chiplet test strategies, heterogeneous
integration as products, datacenter power delivery, or AI accelerator business deals
are NOT relevant here.

3. ANALOG, RF, AND MIXED-SIGNAL CIRCUITS AND INDUSTRY
New data converter architectures (ADCs, DACs), PLL and frequency synthesizer designs,
RF front ends and mmWave circuits, power management ICs (LDOs, DC-DC converters,
PMICs), and precision analog. Business and product news from analog-heavy companies
like TI, ADI, Infineon, Skyworks, Qorvo, Renesas, Microchip, and NXP.

RESEARCH PAPERS are a priority. I want to see notable new papers from IEEE JSSC,
IEEE T-MTT, IEEE TCAS, and arXiv covering: AI/ML for analog design automation,
RF/mmWave circuit techniques, data converters, PLLs, power management, and novel
analog architectures. Papers about AI applied to analog/RF design are the most
valuable.

I do NOT care about: AI chip packaging and test strategies, heterogeneous integration,
chiplet architectures, HBM stacking, datacenter power delivery, digital logic design,
CPU microarchitecture, FPGA/ASIC design flows, digital-only EDA, SoC integration,
networking chips, storage controllers, consumer gadget reviews, gaming benchmarks,
crypto mining, software-only AI/ML news unrelated to chip design, general wireless
communications theory unrelated to circuit design, or general business news that only
mentions chips in passing.
